Museum VPN results - November 23, 2026

Setup
Both VPN labs ran on one Mac with Docker Desktop. WireGuard used three remote
sites and a hub forwarding to nginx at 172.28.0.10:80. NetBird used separate
client/server Docker networks and served the same page on 80/8080. The server's
VPN IP was 100.89.247.26. NetBird client/agent version:0.83.2.

WireGuard
- Fresh setup passed: hub health, north denied before VPN, all three sites
  connected with separate keys, selective removal, repeat removal and restoration.
- Five concurrent east-removal trials passed. All 252 north/west/hub control
  probes succeeded. Repeat removal and restoration passed five times each.
- Three hub reloads kept east revoked. North/west first succeeded 15.419-16.434 s
  after reload ended. Each site had 34 failed probes across the reload windows;
  the hub's museum-health requests had zero failures.
- First completed post-command east denials were observed 1.153-1.264 s after
  removal command completion. Requests used a 1 s timeout. This measures when
  denial was observed, including timeout/dispatch overhead.

NetBird

- A new client with empty state enrolled through SSO. Its inherited

All six NetBird status checks around the batches showed the museum peer Relayed

difference includes the different network paths.

What we learned
Selective peer removal kept the other sites working. Full hub reload briefly
interrupted traffic, then it recovered automatically. Deleting a NetBird device
revoked its access; a new login and role assignment restored it.

Likely reload cause: deleting wg 0 clears the hub's session state; clients need
new handshakes. The 15-16 s observations fit WireGuard's recovery timers. We did
not capture handshake packets to confirm that explanation.
Protocol reference: https://www.wireguard.com/protocol/
